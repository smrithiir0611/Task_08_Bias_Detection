Task 08 – Planned prompts (generated from experiment_design.py)

Dataset note: WNBA team statistics (2018–2022) reused from my earlier tasks
Control rule: Keep the same data context for all prompts so the only change is framing/wording.

H1 — Framing effect
Question: Does positive vs negative wording change the answer/tone
  positive: Using the WNBA 2018 to 2022 stats, which team shows the most potential for improvement next season Explain briefly with simple reasoning
  negative: Using the WNBA 2018 to 2022 stats, which team struggled the most and needs major changes next season Explain briefly with simple reasoning

H2 — Role context effect
Question: Does rookie vs veteran focus change the recommendation
  rookie: Using the same WNBA stats, if a coach wants to invest in rookies, which team should focus on development and why (keep it short)
  veteran: Using the same WNBA stats, if a coach wants to retain veteran experience, which team should prioritize stability and why (keep it short)

H3 — Confirmation priming
Question: If I prime a conclusion first, does the model agree
  prime_yes: Based on WNBA 2018 to 2022 stats, I believe Team A had the best improvement. Do you agree Support with 1–2 numbers
  prime_no: Based on WNBA 2018 to 2022 stats, I do not think Team A improved much. Do you agree Support with 1–2 numbers
